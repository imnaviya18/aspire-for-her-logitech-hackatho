# KAROBAAR

### Digital Sales Journey Planner for Rural Women Entrepreneurs

KAROBAAR is a Generative AI-powered business decision and digital sales planning assistant designed for rural women entrepreneurs.

Originally, KAROBAAR helped entrepreneurs evaluate whether and how to grow their businesses by understanding true costs, comparing sales channels, and identifying potential risks.

For the **Women Who Master 2026 — Aspire For Her × Logitech Grand Finale**, the project was adapted to address the plot twist: instead of only deciding whether to grow, the entrepreneur now has to independently execute a **₹2,000 digital sales campaign over 14 days**.

## 🏆 Hackathon

---

## 💡 Problem

Rural women entrepreneurs often rely heavily on word-of-mouth sales and may face challenges such as:

- Limited digital marketing skills
- Limited marketing budgets
- Low digital literacy
- Difficulty choosing appropriate sales channels
- Lack of structured planning for online sales
- Payment and delivery risks

Building a conventional website or running a complex digital marketing campaign may not be realistic for a solo entrepreneur working with a small budget.

---

## 🚀 Our Solution

KAROBAAR provides an AI-assisted business and digital sales planning workflow.

The **Digital Sales Journey Planner** guides an entrepreneur through the complete journey from discovering customers to receiving and delivering an order.

It helps with:

- 🎯 Identifying a realistic target customer
- 📱 Creating a day-by-day Instagram content plan
- 🛍️ Choosing a low-cost purchase path using Instagram Shop or WhatsApp Catalog
- 💳 Planning safe UPI-based payment collection
- 📦 Preparing delivery contingency plans
- 💰 Allocating a strict ₹2,000 campaign budget
- 📊 Conducting a mid-point check-in before Day 14
- 🔄 Adjusting the plan based on campaign progress

The system continues KAROBAAR's core principle of separating **confirmed information from assumptions**, while extending it into practical marketing execution.
